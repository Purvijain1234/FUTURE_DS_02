# 🔍 Key Insights

## 1️⃣ Month-to-Month Customers Have Highest Churn

Customers with month-to-month contracts showed the highest churn rate compared to one-year and two-year contracts.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Short-term customers are more likely to leave the service.
- Businesses should encourage customers to switch to long-term plans using discounts and loyalty benefits.

---

## 2️⃣ Higher Monthly Charges Increase Churn Probability

Customers with higher monthly charges were more likely to churn than customers with moderate pricing plans.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Expensive plans may reduce customer retention.
- Businesses should optimize pricing strategies and provide better value-added services.

---

## 3️⃣ Fiber Optic Internet Users Contribute Significantly to Churn

Customers using Fiber Optic internet services showed relatively higher churn rates.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Service quality and customer support for Fiber Optic users should be improved.
- Businesses can introduce premium support and retention campaigns for these customers.

---

## 4️⃣ Electronic Check Payment Users Have Higher Churn

Customers using electronic check payment methods contributed heavily to customer churn.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Payment experience may influence customer satisfaction.
- Businesses should encourage automatic payments and digital billing methods.

---

## 5️⃣ Long-Term Customers Show Better Retention

Customers with longer tenure demonstrated stronger loyalty and lower churn probability.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Retaining customers for longer periods increases business stability.
- Loyalty programs and customer engagement strategies should focus on early-stage customers.

---

## 6️⃣ Senior Citizens Show Different Churn Behavior

Senior citizen customers displayed noticeable churn trends compared to non-senior customers.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Businesses should create age-specific support and retention strategies.
- Simplified services and better customer assistance may improve retention.

---

## 7️⃣ Internet Service Distribution Impacts Customer Retention

Internet service type plays an important role in customer retention and satisfaction.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Improving internet service reliability can reduce churn.
- Businesses should monitor service-specific customer feedback regularly.

---

## 8️⃣ Customer Retention Directly Affects Revenue Stability

High churn rates can negatively impact long-term business growth and recurring revenue.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Customer retention strategies are essential for sustainable business performance.
- Retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new customers.
